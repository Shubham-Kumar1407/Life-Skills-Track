# Prevention of Sexual Harassment

## Question What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment is any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, unsafe or humiliated. It can happen in person, online, by phone or in writing. It generally falls into these categories:

**1. Verbal**
- Sexual jokes, comments or remarks about someone's body, clothes or appearance
- Repeated requests for dates or sexual favours
- Asking intrusive questions about someone's personal or sexual life

**2. Non-verbal**
- Making sexual gestures
- Showing or sharing pornography, sexual images, videos or messages
- Sending inappropriate texts, emails or social media messages

**3. Physical**
- Unwanted touching, hugging, patting against someone
- Blocking someone's way or standing too close on purpose
- Sexual assault or any forced physical contact

**4. Misusing Your Position or Power**
- Promising a promotion, a good review or other benefits in return for sexual favours
- Threatening a person with negative consequences, such as a bad review or termination, if they refuse


## What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

**If I face it:**
1. **Say no clearly.** If I feel safe, I will tell the person directly that the behaviour is unwelcome and must stop.
2. **Keep a record.** I will note the date, time, place, what happened and any witnesses, and save messages, emails or screenshots as proof.
3. **Report it.** I will report the incident to my mentor, HR or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC) as per the company's PoSH policy, preferably in writing.
4. **Follow the process.** I will cooperate with the inquiry, knowing that complaints are handled confidentially and that retaliation against the complainant is not allowed.

**If I witness it:**
1. **Do not ignore it.** Staying silent can allow the behaviour to continue.
2. **Support the person.** If it is safe, I will step in or help the person leave the situation, and I will check on them afterwards.
3. **Report it myself** to the appropriate authority if the person is unable to do so, while keeping the matter confidential.
